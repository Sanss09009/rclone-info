Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

This application is a personal OAuth application used by its owner to connect Google Drive with Rclone.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the permissions granted, Rclone may access Google Drive files, folders, and metadata to perform operations requested by the user.

3. Use of Information

Google Drive information is used only for personal file management operations, including listing, uploading, downloading, copying, and synchronizing files.

4. Data Storage

Rclone stores OAuth authentication tokens in its local configuration file on the user's computer. Users should protect this file from unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

The application owner does not sell or share Google Drive data for advertising or unrelated purposes. File transfers are performed through Google APIs as requested by the user.

6. Security

Users are responsible for protecting their Google account, computer, and local Rclone configuration.

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke OAuth access through their Google Account security settings.

8. Contact

For questions about this application, contact the application owner through their GitHub profile.
